Where it started

Oxmere began in 2014 as a two-bench repair shop. Customers kept asking whether we could make them a pair from scratch, so we learned to.

Today twenty-six craftspeople cut, last, stitch and finish every pair on site. We buy hides from tanneries we visit, and we still repair what we sell.
