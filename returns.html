Return & Exchange

Changed your mind or need another size? Here's how.

Exchanges

You can exchange unworn items for a different size or style within 14 days of delivery. Exchanges are free inside Dhaka.

Returns

Unworn items in their original box can be returned within 7 days for a refund to your original payment method. Refunds take 5–7 working days to appear.

What we can't accept

  • Items that show signs of wear, including creasing or marked soles
  • Made-to-order and personalised items
  • Sale items, which can be exchanged for size only

How to start

Message us with your order number, or bring the item to the flagship store with your receipt.
